Summary of Benefits
Current Issue	Proposed Solution
Mixed concerns in single file (guardrail_and_verification_handlers.py)	Split by lifecycle stage (pre-execution, verification, recovery)
Tool-level guardrails mixed with turn handlers	Separate execution_guardrails/ from turn_handlers/
Recovery logic scattered across files	Consolidate into recovery/healing/
Unclear ownership of functions	Clear naming: _validation.py, _healing.py, etc.

Phase 1: Move definitions and routing to subdirectories
Phase 2: Split guardrail_and_verification_handlers.py into turn handlers + verification
Phase 3: Extract execution guardrails from tools.py
